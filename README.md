# Bashmon: Glitch Version

Deep within the forgotten corners of the digital world, something has gone terribly wrong.

A mysterious glitch has awakened 42 peculiar creatures, each born from the chaos of technology, programming, and the internet. From corrupted memory and runaway processes to suspicious network packets and ancient hardware, these creatures now roam freely across the system.

They are known as **Bashmon**.

For generations, the digital world remained peaceful. But now, its balance has been disturbed, and a new generation of aspiring Debuggers must rise to the challenge.

Choose your Bashmon, face your rival, and prove that you have what it takes to become the ultimate Debugger.

The journey won't be easy. There are bugs to squash, loops to escape, and some particularly stubborn processes to terminate.

After all, there are 42 answers to the universe. Unfortunately, some of them have segmentation faults.

**Gotta fix 'em all!**
